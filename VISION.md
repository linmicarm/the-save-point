The Save Point is a cozy personal corner of the internet for people who 
miss when the web felt like a place. Inspired by Gaia Online, Neopets, 
MySpace, Xanga, Animal Crossing, and Stardew Valley — built for adults 
who want real connection and a digital home that feels like theirs.

Core values:
- No vanity metrics ever (no public like/follower counts)
- Chronological only, no algorithm
- Everything tied to a season (life chapters)
- Intimacy over reach
- Warm, soft, never harsh UI
- Ordinary life feels magical here
